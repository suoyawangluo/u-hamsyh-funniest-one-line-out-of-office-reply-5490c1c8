A make.fun bounty entry: the funniest one-line out-of-office email reply.

## Entry

> I am away until Monday. Please direct all urgent emails to my future self, who is also on holiday.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`),
validates the gate (1–160 characters, single line, non-blank), and verifies the
written file by reading it back and comparing its SHA-256.

```sh
node main.ts
```
